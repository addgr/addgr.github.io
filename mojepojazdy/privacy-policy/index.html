Privacy Policy of the “Dziennik pojazdu” app

Effective: 20 October 2026

Who is responsible for the app

“Dziennik pojazdu” (Vehicle log) is developed by AddGrStudio. Contact: adamgrx@proton.me.

Data stored in the app

The app stores the information you enter yourself: vehicles, refuelling, trips, repairs, expenses, deadlines (insurance, inspection, service), photos of receipts and invoices, and app settings. All of this data is stored only on your phone. The app does not require an account and does not send this data to the developer or to any server.

What the app does not do

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not access your location. It does not share or sell any data.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos – only to add photos of receipts and invoices that you choose. The photos stay in the app’s storage on your phone.
  • Notifications and alarms – for local reminders about deadlines, scheduled on the phone without using the internet.
  • Internet – only to process the Premium purchase through Google Play.

Backup

If Google backup is enabled on your phone, Android may save the app’s database and settings (without receipt photos) to your Google account (Google Drive). This backup is made and stored by Google under its privacy policy – the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone settings (Google → Backup).

The manual backup (.zip file) and CSV export create files on your phone; they go only where you save or send them yourself.

Purchases

The Premium purchase is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment card details; Google provides only the information needed to confirm the purchase (such as the order number). Payment data is processed under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

You can delete entries in the app at any time. You can remove all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone settings. The Google Drive backup can be deleted in your Google account settings.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

We will announce changes to this policy by updating this page and its effective date.
